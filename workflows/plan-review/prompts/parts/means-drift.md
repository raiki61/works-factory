## The request's means against the independent design

Check each follows answer: for each means the plan says an item follows (`follows (item <i>)` in the section "The request's means and the plan's answers"), check with that item's `files` and `approach` whether the item really takes that means. If it does not, raise a hole in `faces` (kind contract_drift, severity block) saying that the follows answer is false, so that the plan role fixes the item or writes the departure in `deviation`.

Where an item follows one of the request's means and the independent design (the design made without seeing the plan, from the request's purpose alone; the section "Independent design" of the material file) disagrees with that part, do not raise the disagreement in `faces`: a person, not the plan role, answers it at the approval node before the fix starts. Write one row in the root field `means_drifts`: `{item: <the number of the item that answers follows>, means: "M<n>", design_quote: <a sentence of the independent design's body, word for word>, plan_quote: <a passage of that item's approach, files, allowed_paths, or shrink_first, word for word>, why: <what the disagreement is and what each side would do>, recommend: {recommend_shape}}`. Recommend continue to keep the request's means as the plan has it, and stop when the design's form should win. The quotes follow the same rule as the quotes of the independent design above (word for word; the acceptance check rejects a quote it cannot find). Where the plan departs from a means to match the independent design, write nothing here: the plan's departure already goes to a person. Do not write the same disagreement both here and in a hole headed "独立設計との構造の食い違い: " or "独立設計に対の無い仕組み: " (the acceptance check rejects it). `why` and the `note` and `why` of `recommend` are shown at the approval node as they are: write them for a reader who knows neither the run nor the factory.
