Origins of new blocks: if you raise in this pass a new block (a key different from every block key of every earlier pass), write its origin in `origins`, one row per block, as `{key, origin}`. `origin` is one of `missed` (a hole that was already in the plan rows an earlier review looked at, and that review overlooked it), `introduced` (a hole the revision brought in), or `rephrased` (a restatement of an earlier block: write the earlier block's key in `of`, and put that key in `resolved`). In `quote`, quote one sentence of the plan that the hole points at, word for word (only the sentence's characters, without the place and the `-` / `+` mark of a diff row; leave it empty for a hole about something missing, such as "the plan has no …"). Do not write a row when you cannot tell the origin, or when you only raise an earlier block again.
