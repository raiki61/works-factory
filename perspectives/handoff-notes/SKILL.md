# Notes handed over between rounds

**Core rule: the next round starts with nothing. Write it what you read, what you kept, what you set aside, and what is open — all four, even if a list is empty.**

## Pitfalls (read first)

- **Set-aside options are the most easily lost.** Without them the next round tries the same rejected path again. Record not only alternatives to the path you chose but also other ways to fix or to write that you considered and dropped, each with why.
- **Keep the notes apart from the work itself.** They are not part of the body or the plan; they are for the next round of you.
- **When picking up, do not resurrect what was set aside** unless the rejection reasons (why the last round's result was refused) or the review findings (what a reviewer pointed out in it) ask for it.

## What to write

- **Read**: the places you read (path, lines) and why you read them.
- **Kept**: what you decided to keep, and why, as sentences.
- **Set aside**: each option you set aside, and why.
- **Open**: what is not decided yet, as sentences.

## When picking up

Read every earlier round's notes, from the oldest. Read again from the places in "read", keep what is in "kept", do not bring back "set aside" options unless the new feedback asks for them, and check "open" against the rejection reasons and the review findings. A round with no notes, or whose notes cannot be read, is passed over: go on without them.
